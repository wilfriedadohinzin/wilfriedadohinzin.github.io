I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Illinois, where my research focused on labor economics and development economics. I am broadly interested in questions related to state capacity building in developing economies, the measurement of teacher quality, the impact of job security in public-sector labor markets, housing policy in the context of immigration enforcement, and the impact of childbearing on labor market outcomes.

I am a Senior Associate in the Labor & Employment practice at Charles River Associates. Within this space, I am particularly interested in emerging issues such as the role of AI in employment discrimination and bias litigation, as well as monopsony power and anticompetitive behavior in labor markets. I originally hail from Benin, grew up in its most vibrant city, Cotonou, but also lived in various West African cities such as Lomé, Accra, and Ouagadougou, and my CV can be found here — feel free to reach me at wilfriedadoh@gmail.com.
