The Impacts of Job Security and Tenure Reforms on Teacher and Student Outcomes
Abstract

I study merit‑based job security for public‑sector teachers. Offering permanent contracts reduces exits, especially among high‑quality teachers, and modestly improves student learning; effects are weaker in rural and low‑SES settings. Composition changes increase average teacher quality.

The Effects of Immigration Enforcement on the Rental Market: Evidence from the Secure Communities Program
with Alok Ranjan
Abstract

Enhanced interior immigration enforcement decreases rental prices in targeted areas, consistent with out‑migration and demand reallocation.
